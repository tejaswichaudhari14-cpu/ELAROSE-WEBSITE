THE SAFETY LAYER

Smart technology, with the wearer in control.

SENSE

Wearable sensors can collect signals such as heart-rate-related and skin-conductance changes.

CONNECT

Bluetooth Low Energy connects the wearable event to the paired smartphone.

INTERPRET

The app combines context and signals; a single biometric spike is not treated as proof of danger.

RESPOND

User-confirmed emergency workflows can notify chosen contacts and share location.

PRESS→LIGHT / VIBRATE→PRESS AGAIN→ALERT

Smart Confirm™ is designed to reduce accidental activation while keeping the user in control.

TRUST

Privacy by design.

MINIMIZE

Collect only what the feature needs.

CONSENT

User controls contacts, location and optional features.

ON-DEVICE WHERE FEASIBLE

Prefer local processing for sensitive signals where technically practical.

CONTROL

Clear settings for data access, sharing and deletion.
